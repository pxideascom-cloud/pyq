Geophysics stream (set aside)

210 questions, 420 marks, 2014–2020.

Until 2020 the GG paper printed two alternative technical streams and a candidate answered one of them: Section 1 for geology, Section 2 for geophysics. These are the Section 2 questions. They are excluded from the analysis and from the weightage tables because a geology candidate never answered them, and including them would distort every per-topic figure.

They are kept here rather than deleted for two reasons. Geophysical concepts still appear inside the common Part A and inside the single undivided section that the 2021 and later papers use — seismic velocity, gravity anomalies, resistivity — and those questions are in the analysis. And the 2021 change means Section 2 has no successor, so this page is the historical record of a paper format that no longer exists.
